WHAT IT DOES

Better coverage without losing a single final answer

Dual-Lobe is designed to reduce tunnel vision on difficult tasks. When a problem has independent angles, it explores more than one path at once, then brings those paths back together, repairs gaps, checks for contradictions, and delivers one coherent result.

release candidate
01Looks beyond the obvious path

Dual-Lobe actively searches for separate angles, missing context, alternative explanations, and independent pieces of work instead of committing too early to one line of thought.

02Works on useful parts in parallel

When parts of a task can be handled independently, it advances them at the same time so broader work does not automatically mean slower work.

03Rebuilds everything into one answer

It combines the strongest useful material, removes duplication, resolves conflicts, restores missing pieces, and turns separate work into a single coherent result.

04Checks claims before delivery

It looks for unsupported statements, contradictions, missing proof, overconfidence, incomplete task coverage, and claims about actions or artifacts that are not actually evidenced.

WHY IT MATTERS

Designed against tunnel vision

  • Explores independent angles instead of relying on one narrow reasoning path.
  • Surfaces missing prerequisites, failure modes, tradeoffs, and overlooked questions.
  • Combines multiple lines of useful work without forcing the user to reconcile them manually.
  • Repairs obvious gaps before the answer is presented.
  • Keeps one final answer rather than exposing fragmented competing outputs.
TRUST

Evidence-aware answers

  • Distinguishes “no deception detected” from “verified true.”
  • Does not treat a model's own statement as proof of itself.
  • Checks whether claimed actions, files, tool results, and artifacts are actually supported by evidence.
  • Flags unresolved claims instead of silently presenting them as proven.
  • Can carry one checked, unified result forward through longer multi-step work.
